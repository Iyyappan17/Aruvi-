Monthly Old Age Pension Scheme

Monthly pension support for registered unorganized workers on reaching 60 years of age.

Service Overview & Benefits

Lifelong monthly social security pension credited directly to the worker's bank account upon reaching 60 years of age.

Scheme Purpose: Monthly pension support for registered unorganized workers on reaching 60 years of age.

Who May Need This Service (Eligibility)

Registered welfare board members who have completed 60 years of age with continuous membership.

Verified Required Documents (5)

Please ensure you carry the original copies of the following documents when applying.

  • Original Welfare Board Card
  • Ration Card (Smart Card)
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Active Bank Passbook
  • Passport Photo and Direct Photo

Application Steps & Guidance

1

Gather Original Documents

Collect the required certificates from the checklist above, ensuring Aadhaar details match your bank records.

2

Complete Online Request Form

Click 'Apply Now' below to fill out the service-specific application form with your details.

3

Verification at Aruvi E-Sevai Maiyam

Visit our centre in Kovilpatti or call our desk to finalize official processing and obtain your submission acknowledgment.
