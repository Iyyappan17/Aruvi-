About Aruvi

About Aruvi E-Sevai Maiyam

Aruvi E-Sevai Maiyam is a community-focused public service centre based in Kovilpatti. We assist workers, students, senior citizens, and families in accessing authentic government welfare schemes.

Accurate Eligibility Check

We verify your age, trade, and document criteria before you apply, eliminating unnecessary rejections.

Clear Document Checklists

Every service is accompanied by a transparent checklist so you know exactly which original documents are required.

Senior Citizen Friendly

Courteous, respectful in-person guidance for unorganized laborers and elders with Tamil communication.
